Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2025


InboxWhiz is a browser-side tool that runs locally on your browser. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data.

What Data We Access

InboxWhiz operates entirely inside your own browser and interacts directly with the Gmail page you already have open. All processing happens locally.

We do not use the Gmail API and we do not request or store authorization tokens.

Data Storage

InboxWhiz does not store any email content, credentials, analytics, or personal information on external servers. No data is transmitted off your device. Any temporary state (for UI selections, filters, etc.) is stored only in your browser's local storage.

Third-Party Sharing

InboxWhiz does not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

Uninstall Behavior

When you remove InboxWhiz from your browser, all locally stored extension data is removed by the browser automatically.

Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page, so please review it regularly.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at inboxwhiz.help@gmail.com
